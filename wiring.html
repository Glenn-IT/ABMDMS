Wireless Motion Detection with ESP32 & SIM800L

Upgraded to ESP32 Wi-Fi. Motion events and SMS status reports are posted directly to your XAMPP backend over the local Wi-Fi network — no USB tethering cord or PowerShell serial bridge required.

Wireless Architecture • No USB Cord Needed

The ESP32 runs standalone on your 5V HW-131 breadboard power adapter. When motion is detected, it triggers the MAX98357A I2S speaker siren, texts via SIM800L (Hardware UART2), and transmits HTTP POST data over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi straight into record_motion.php and record_sms.php.

01What you need on the desk

ESP32 Dev Module ×1 · NodeMCU-32S / ESP32-WROOM

Powered via VIN from HW-131 5V rail. Direct Wi-Fi HTTP client & Hardware UART2.

HW-131 Power Module ×1 · MB-102 compatible

Plugs onto breadboard end. Sets both rails to 5 V. Powers ESP32 VIN, SIM800L, and PIRs.

12 V DC Wall Adapter ×1 · 1 A to 2 A rating

Plugs into HW-131 DC barrel jack (5.5mm × 2.1mm center positive).

SIM800L V2.2 (UNV) ×1 · 5 V board

Communicates with ESP32 via Hardware UART2 (GPIO 16 RX / GPIO 17 TX).

HC-SR501 PIR Sensors ×3 · ROOMA / ROOMB / ROOMC

Powered by 5V rail. Sensor OUT is 3.3V logic — 100% safe to plug directly into ESP32 GPIOs!

MAX98357A I2S Amp + Speaker ×1 · 3W Class-D Audio

LRC → GPIO 25, BCLK → GPIO 26, DIN → GPIO 27. Connects to 4Ω/8Ω speaker for loud sirens.

1000 µF capacitor ×1 · 16 V or higher

Sits across SIM800L 5Vin and GND in parallel to buffer 2 A GSM transmit bursts.

Breadboard + Jumpers 830-point · M-F & M-M

Male-to-female for module/sensor headers; male-to-male for rails & ground links.

02Every connection, pin by pin

Clean and simple. The ESP32 is powered via VIN from the regulated 5V rail. The PIR signal wires connect directly to GPIO inputs.

From ComponentTo ESP32 / RailFunction & Notes
ESP32 VIN HW-131 5V (bottom + rail) Powers the ESP32 on-board 3.3V regulator from the 5V power supply.
ESP32 GND common ground (bottom − rail) Common ground shared across all boards and sensors.
PIR 1 OUT (ROOMC) ESP32 GPIO 13 Direct wire. HC-SR501 outputs 3.3V HIGH signal — safe for ESP32.
PIR 2 OUT (ROOMA) ESP32 GPIO 12 Direct wire to GPIO 12.
PIR 3 OUT (ROOMB) ESP32 GPIO 14 Direct wire to GPIO 14.
MAX98357A LRC (WS) ESP32 GPIO 25 I2S Word Select Clock — Left/Right audio channel sync.
MAX98357A BCLK ESP32 GPIO 26 I2S Bit Clock — timing clock for digital audio bitstream.
MAX98357A DIN ESP32 GPIO 27 I2S Serial Data Input — sends digital synthesized wave samples.
MAX98357A VIN & GND HW-131 5V & common GND Powers 3W amplifier from 5V rail; shares common ground with ESP32.
MAX98357A SPK (+ / −) Speaker (+ / −) Terminals Direct connection to 4Ω or 8Ω speaker.
All PIR VCC pins HW-131 5V (bottom + rail) Powers all PIR sensors from the 5V bus.
All PIR GND pins common ground (− rail) Taps into bottom ground rail.
SIM800L TXD ESP32 GPIO 16 (RX2) Hardware UART2 RX. Direct connection (or via 1k/2k divider).
SIM800L RXD ESP32 GPIO 17 (TX2) Hardware UART2 TX. ESP32 3.3V TX drives SIM800L RX reliably.
SIM800L RST ESP32 GPIO 4 Hardware reset pin (Active LOW).
SIM800L 5Vin HW-131 5V (top + rail) Powered from top 5V rail. Transmit burst pulls 2 A — buffered by 1000µF capacitor.
SIM800L GND HW-131 − and ESP32 GND Must share common ground with ESP32.
1000 µF Capacitor Across SIM800L 5Vin & GND In parallel across 5Vin and GND. Stripe leg to ground.

03Wireless Data Flow (Before vs. After)

Direct Wi-Fi Transmission

PIR Sensor → ESP32 → SIM800L → Your Phone (SMS)
PIR Sensor → ESP32 → [Wi-Fi HTTP POST] → PHP API (XAMPP) → MySQL → Web & Android Dashboard

The ESP32 eliminates the middleman script! You no longer need serial_reader.ps1 or a COM port open.

04Pre-Power Checklist

01Check the HW-131 jumpers — verify both are seated firmly at 5V.
02Multimeter continuity check on the ground rails — verify ESP32 GND, SIM800L GND, and PIR GND are unified.
03Confirm the 1000 µF capacitor polarity across SIM800L power rails (striped leg to GND).
04Confirm VDD on the SIM800L is unconnected.
05Verify Wi-Fi credentials (WIFI_SSID, WIFI_PASSWORD) and your PC's IP (SERVER_IP) in esp32_motion_sensor.ino.

05Troubleshooting

SymptomProbable CauseResolution
Wi-Fi connection fails Incorrect SSID/Password or 5GHz network ESP32 only supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Check your router SSID and password.
HTTP POST returns error (-1 or connection refused) Wrong PC IP or Windows Firewall blocking Port 80 Find your PC's IPv4 via ipconfig and update SERVER_IP. Ensure Apache is running in XAMPP and allowed in Windows Firewall.
SIM800L no reply to AT commands GPIO 16/17 swapped or power insufficient Ensure SIM800L TXD → GPIO 16 and RXD → GPIO 17. Verify HW-131 has 12V adapter plugged in.
Sensor false-triggers on SMS send GSM RF burst coupling into PIR dome Position the GSM antenna at least 15–20 cm away from the PIR sensor domes.
